Tip 2 Tip

1,000–1,200 KM 5 DAYS SELF-SUPPORTED

Start at The Hawk Beach, with the Atlantic behind you. Finish at Meat Cove, about 1,000–1,200 km of Nova Scotia later. What happens in between is largely up to you.

Carry what you need. Find food, water and somewhere to sleep along the way. No support vehicles. No private supply drops. Just you, your bike, five days and a very long way north.

Tip 2 Tip grew from a few friends riding end-to-end in 2020 to its first race in 2021. Since then, each edition has taken riders on a different journey across Nova Scotia.

When?

August 28 – September 2, 2027.

Registration for all races opens in January/February 2027.

Race Formats

Take on the full Tip 2 Tip route solo or as a pair. Choose your checkpoints and make your own way across Nova Scotia.

SOLO

Your route. Your ride.

Ride independently, choosing how to connect the checkpoints and when to stop along the way.

PAIRS

Two riders. One shared adventure.

Work together, draft one another, and decide as a team how to connect the checkpoints and manage the ride.

Past edition: Tip 2 Tip 2025. This is not the upcoming race course.

The Route

There is no prescribed route. Riders are given a series of checkpoints scattered across the province and choose how to connect them. The shortest route isn’t always the fastest, and the best idea at noon can look considerably worse at 2 a.m.

Checkpoints change between editions; use the information provided for your race when planning your ride.
